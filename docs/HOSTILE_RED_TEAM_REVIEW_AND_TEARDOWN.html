2. Hostile Competitor Teardown: Why They Break on the Shop Floor

Competitor A: DriftGuard (ESP32 + Thermocouples + Pressure Transducer + Hopper RH)

  • The Cavity Pressure Fallacy: Tapping mold pressure requires drilling into hardened tool steel, voiding toolmaker warranties (₹2L–₹5L per mold). ESP32 ADCs suffer massive EMI from nearby hydraulic pump motors.
  • Hopper Air RH ≠ Resin Moisture: Relative humidity of ambient air inside a hopper does not equal internal bound moisture. Engineering polymers (PA66, PBT) require moisture down to 0.02% (200 ppm). Ambient RH triggers massive false positives during monsoons.
  • Zero Actionability: Stops at "Drift detected"—gives a red graph and walks away.

Mentor Verdict: Dead on arrival. Generic hackathon IoT board.

Competitor B: Team 1 (₹10,700 ESP32 Kit + Drift Countdown + Bilingual App)

  • Unrealistic BOM Costing: An industrial melt pressure transducer (Gefran/Dynisco) alone costs ₹45,000–₹1,10,000. A ₹10,700 kit relies on uncalibrated consumer thermistors that drift faster than the machine barrel.
  • The "Drift Countdown" Illusion: Injection molding drift is non-linear (thermal saturation, regrind fluctuations). A linear countdown timer is physically meaningless.

Mentor Verdict: Brittle under questioning. Sensor part numbers will expose BOM claims.

Competitor C: MoldSense (Phone-First Software + Screen-Photo OCR + QR Recipe Lock)

  • Cycle-Time Ergonomics: Automotive cycle time is 18–40 seconds. An operator managing 2 presses, degating, and trimming parts with oily heat-resistant gloves cannot snap an OCR photo every 30 seconds.
  • Optical Glare & CRT Tube Flicker: Shop-floor grease and glare cause OCR misreads (e.g. 235°C read as 285°C), triggering false halts.
  • Latency Kills Containment: Snapping photos every 30 minutes means 60–90 defect cycles have already dropped into the bin before detection.

Mentor Verdict: High demo appeal, zero operational viability.

Competitor D: Trevoga (₹25,200 DIN-Rail ESP32-S3 + TinyML + SSR Shot Diversion)

  • The Mold Crash Disaster: Actuating a scrap chute diverter via microcontroller SSR without a certified Euromap 67 PLC safety handshake risks a catastrophic mold crash (₹15L+ tool damage and 3 weeks line stoppage) if the flapper mis-times by 300ms.
  • Vent Gas Sensor Clogging: Mold vent deposits (plasticizers, waxes) foul gas sensors within 48–72 operating hours.
  • The Black-Box Anomaly Trap: TinyML gives an "anomaly score" (0.88), not a corrective temperature or pressure setpoint.

Mentor Verdict: Dangerous automation hack. Confuses tolerating scrap with preventing scrap.
