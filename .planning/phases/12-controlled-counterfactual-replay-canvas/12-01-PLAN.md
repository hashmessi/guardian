# Plan 12-01: Dual-Stream Counterfactual Replay Engine (CRPL-01)

## Objective
Implement `src/process_guardian/counterfactual/` containing deterministic dual-mode simulation replay models and engine comparing Guardian OFF (Status Quo) vs Guardian ON (Dynamic Quality Reaction) on identical drift trajectories.

## Scope
- Models: `src/process_guardian/counterfactual/models.py`
  - `CounterfactualStreamPoint`: `cycle_index`, `barrel_temp`, `injection_pressure`, `defect_risk`, `is_defective`, `state`, `action_applied`, `quarantine_status`.
  - `CounterfactualRun`: `mode`, `total_cycles`, `suspect_cycles`, `scrap_parts_count`, `quarantine_parts_count`, `total_financial_loss`, `stream`.
  - `CounterfactualComparison`: `guardian_off`, `guardian_on`, `suspect_lot_reduction_pct`, `scrap_reduction_pct`, `net_avoided_loss`, `timeline_divergence_cycle`, `epistemic_disclaimer`.
- Engine: `src/process_guardian/counterfactual/engine.py`
  - `CounterfactualReplayEngine`:
    - Generates identical baseline (Cycles 1–71).
    - Injects drift at Cycle 72.
    - Simulates Guardian OFF: uncorrected drift, wide SPC threshold, delayed quarantine (80+ cycles, 30+ scrap parts).
    - Simulates Guardian ON: early detection, recommendation, intervention at C-85, stabilization at C-90, QC check at C-92, release at C-93 (21 cycles, 3 scrap parts).
    - Calculates metrics: suspect lot reduction ($\ge 75\%$), scrap reduction ($\ge 85\%$), avoided cost in INR.
- Unit Tests: `tests/test_counterfactual_engine.py`
  - Test pre-drift trajectory identity (Cycles 1–71 match exactly).
  - Test timeline divergence starts at intervention cycle.
  - Test suspect lot reduction exceeds 75%.
  - Test scrap reduction and positive net avoided financial loss.
  - Test Level 4 epistemic disclaimer presence.

## Verification
- Run `pytest tests/test_counterfactual_engine.py` and confirm all tests pass.
