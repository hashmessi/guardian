# Plan 13.1: Deterministic Scenario Registry & Disturbance Injection Framework

## Goal
Establish data-driven scenario schemas, models, and a deterministic registry of 10 industrial operating scenarios covering thermal drift, pressure decay, moisture excursion, clerical recipe mismatch, mold spray maintenance, compound multi-signal drift, normal variation, abstention safety gates, failed recovery holds, and successful recovery release.

## Requirements
- Data-driven scenario definitions rather than hardcoded code paths (`architecture.md`).
- Calibrated disturbance patterns: `linear_drift`, `step_shift`, `moisture_surge`, `checkpoint_overdue`.
- Deterministic random seeds and cycle ranges.
- Full pipeline integration through `ClosedLoopOrchestrator` and `InjectionMoldingSimulator`.

## Tasks
1. Define Pydantic models: `DisturbanceSpec`, `OperationalOverrideSpec`, `ExpectedOutcome`, `NarrativeMilestone`, `ScenarioDefinition`.
2. Construct `ScenarioRegistry` with 10 calibrated industrial scenarios:
   - SCN-01-THERMAL (Hero)
   - SCN-02-PRESSURE (Hero)
   - SCN-03-MOISTURE (Hero)
   - SCN-04-RECIPE (Hero)
   - SCN-05-MOLD-SPRAY (Hero)
   - SCN-06-COMPOUND (Hero Boss)
   - SCN-07-NORMAL-VAR (Robustness)
   - SCN-08-LOW-CONF-ABSTAIN (Safety Gate)
   - SCN-09-FAILED-INTERVENTION (Safety Hold)
   - SCN-10-RECOVERY-RELEASE (Full Lifecycle)
3. Implement `ScenarioRunner` to step through simulator events and run unified Guardian lifecycle.
