# Plan 10-02: Real-Time Cost of Waiting vs Cost of Acting Decision Evaluator (ECON-02)

## Objective
Implement the real-time Cost of Waiting vs Cost of Acting decision evaluator ($ExpectedLoss_{wait} - Cost_{act} = DecisionValue$) with an explicit economic intervention threshold in `src/process_guardian/economics/`. Prevent premature line adjustments when $DecisionValue \le 0$, trigger intervention recommendations when $DecisionValue > 0$, while enforcing strict safety invariants ($P_{risk} \ge 0.75$ critical lock and confidence < 0.65 abstention).

## Scope
- Module: `src/process_guardian/economics/models.py`
  - Define `DecisionValueBreakdown`, `EconomicActionDecision` enum, and lookahead parameters.
- Module: `src/process_guardian/economics/engine.py`
  - Implement `CostOfWaitingEvaluator`:
    - Project drift risk trajectory $P_{defect}(t)$ over horizon $H$.
    - Calculate $Cost_{act} = (N_{stabilization} \times CoPQ_{direct}) + OperatorTuningOverhead$.
    - Calculate $ExpectedLoss_{wait} = \sum P_{defect}(t) \times CoPQ_{total} + H \times SortingCost$.
    - Calculate signed $DecisionValue = ExpectedLoss_{wait} - Cost_{act}$.
    - Determine action decision (`INTERVENE_NOW`, `MONITOR_AND_WAIT`, `CRITICAL_QUALITY_LOCK`, `ABSTAIN_UNCERTAINTY`).
- Integrate with `InterventionOptimizer` or recommendation schemas so candidates include economic evaluation context.
- Unit Tests: `tests/test_decision_economics.py`
  - Verify low drift produces negative Decision Value and recommends `MONITOR_AND_WAIT`.
  - Verify severe drift produces positive Decision Value and recommends `INTERVENE_NOW`.
  - Verify critical safety threshold override forces intervention even if horizon is truncated.
  - Verify low model confidence triggers `ABSTAIN_UNCERTAINTY`.
