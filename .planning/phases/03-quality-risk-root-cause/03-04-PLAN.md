# Phase 3 Plan 04: Phase 3 Integration & End-to-End Quality Risk Benchmark

## Goal
Integrate the Quality Risk Model, Quality Contract Evaluator, and Root Cause Analyzer into an end-to-end processing pipeline, and benchmark risk escalation on the primary demo scenario and multi-regime simulations (QRSK-01..04, ROOT-01..03).

## Requirements Addressed
- Full Phase 3 integration and benchmark verification across all 7 requirements (`QRSK-01`, `QRSK-02`, `QRSK-03`, `QRSK-04`, `ROOT-01`, `ROOT-02`, `ROOT-03`).

## Proposed Files
- `src/process_guardian/quality/pipeline.py`: `QualityEngine` unifying feature extraction, risk prediction, contract evaluation, and root-cause analysis into a single `evaluate(record, fingerprint, drift_output)` method.
- `tests/test_quality_e2e.py`: Integration test verifying risk probability escalation ($LOW \to MEDIUM \to HIGH$) during injected thermal drift and correct root-cause attribution to Zone 3 temperature.

## Verification
- Run `pytest tests/test_quality_e2e.py -v`.
